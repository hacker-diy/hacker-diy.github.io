Privacy Policy for MC Watch

Effective date: August 26, 2026 · by Artem Dorofeev

Overview

MC Watch respects your privacy. MC Watch does not require a user account and does not collect, sell, rent or share personal information.

Data Collection

MC Watch does not collect personal data from users. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

Local Device Data

MC Watch may store application preferences, such as language and interface settings, locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Local Network

MC Watch communicates with compatible Magic Control hardware over a local network. Information exchanged between MC Watch and Magic Control is used only to provide the application's hardware-related functionality.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch companion communicates with the iPhone application and compatible Magic Control hardware as required to provide PEEK and FORCE functionality.

Third Parties

MC Watch does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes. The current version will remain available on this page.

Contact and Support

For questions about MC Watch or this Privacy Policy, visit the MC Watch Support page.
