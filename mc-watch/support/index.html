About MC Watch

MC Watch is the Apple Watch companion application for compatible Magic Control hardware. It provides quick access to PEEK and FORCE functions from Apple Watch and communicates with Magic Control over the local network.

Before you start

  • Make sure MC Watch is installed on the paired iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Make sure the iPhone is connected to the local network provided by the compatible Magic Control device when hardware-dependent functions are used.
  • Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled on the iPhone and Apple Watch.

Supported functions

PEEK receives values on Apple Watch when connected to compatible Magic Control hardware.

FORCE lets you select a configured preset, enter a value and send the command to the connected Magic Control device.

Need help?

If you experience an installation, connection, Apple Watch, PEEK or FORCE issue, open a support request and include your iPhone model, iOS version, Apple Watch model, watchOS version, MC Watch version and a short description of the issue.
